The problem

Connection breaks in the gap between people, not inside one person.

Autistic adults are lonelier

A systematic review found loneliness is substantially higher among autistic adults.

Mixed conversations lose meaning

When autistic and non-autistic people pass on a story, details get lost faster, and so does rapport.

The double empathy problem: the gap runs both ways.
