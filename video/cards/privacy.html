Privacy and data protection

Private by design, not by setting.

Invitation-only circles

Every request checks membership. Outsiders learn nothing.

Reading help is private

Only the reader sees it. The sender’s words never change.

Calls aren’t recorded

Captions and interpreter notes vanish when you leave.

Recordings are deleted

Voice and video clips are erased once turned into words, or within 24 hours.

No diagnosis, no mood tracking

We never ask for one or infer one. Delete your account any time.

Your key, your data

Your own AI key stays in your browser. Logs hold no message content.

Designed around data-protection principles: data minimisation, purpose limitation, storage limits and the right to erasure. AI features send text to the AI provider only when you use them. Not a certification.
