How it’s different

Why not just WhatsApp with Meta AI?

General messaging + AI assistantInTune, built for communicating differently
AI helps you write your own messagesInterprets both ways: plain words, what they’re asking, whether to reply, what’s unclear
You copy AI suggestions yourselfYou approve the exact words and audience; AI never sends, and a lost “not”, time or name is flagged
The same assistant for everyoneBuilt around each person: their phrases, “How to talk with me”, tone tags, energy status
Group chats and calls as usualPlans that fit everyone’s stated needs, and calls with a private live interpreter

WhatsApp and Meta AI are great for everyday chat. This compares design goals; it isn’t a feature audit.
