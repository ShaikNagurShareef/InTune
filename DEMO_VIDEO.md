# Demo video script (2:45)

Record on https://intune-eta.vercel.app with two browsers side by side: **Maya** on the left, and the other person on the right (password `InTune-demo-2026`).

Before recording:
- Open every AI moment once as a warm-up, so the models are ready.
- Use Chrome and a real microphone for the call.
- Keep the demo banner visible. It is honest that the people are fictional.

## If something is slow on the day

- The AI falls back between models automatically. If a translation takes more than about 15 s, cut the wait in editing and don't fake the result.
- Captions need Chrome, Edge or Safari. If the microphone isn't picked up, show **Say it for me** and signals instead; they don't depend on speech.
